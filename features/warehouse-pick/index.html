Supercharge your warehouse

Elevate Your Current Operations#

Improve material and process flow orchestration, optimization and visibility for your human-centric warehouses.

The largest problem most warehouses face today is dead-time—the unproductive time spent traveling between locations. Dead-time often accounts for more than 35% of operations, quietly draining profitability. Our engine eliminates this drain, enabling faster, more accurate and flexible operations to maximize your warehouse throughput.

Key Operational Benefits#

  • Increase Warehouse Velocity: Less dead-time means more time fulfilling jobs and driving revenue.
  • Stay on Top of Operations: Dynamic job allocation and manpower planning executed in close to real-time.
  • Built to Adapt: A continuous learning engine ensures IRIS adapts to your ever-changing operations to provide what works best.

Core Capabilities#

🧠 Optimization#

Make the most of your existing warehouse assets.

Path#

Produces an optimal picking path for a large number of orders to reduce traveling time, increase the number of picks, and accelerate warehouse velocity. It seamlessly scales to handle complex configurations with a large number of aisles and cross-aisles per layout.

Batch#

Intelligently groups orders—especially high-frequency, shorter e-commerce orders—allowing items to be picked simultaneously in a single trip. By reducing total travel distance, it minimizes order-picking costs while drastically improving throughput efficiency.

Slot#

Enables predictive pre-positioning of inventory based on demand and movement forecasts. Fast-moving items are placed in readily accessible locations ahead of incoming demand spikes, reducing picking cycles and ensuring strict adherence to customer service level agreements (SLAs) during peak seasons.

🚀 Orchestration#

Execute operations effectively across various functions.

Streamlined Cross-Functionality: Safely streamline activities across multiple operational functions effectively without requiring additional resources.

  • System-Agnostic: Easily deploy and integrate our suite with your current operational infrastructure or your orchestration tool of choice.
  • API-First Design: Fully extensible through clean APIs. From cloud environments to on-premise infrastructure–if it has an API, we have you covered.
  • Zero Ongoing Human Intervention: Runs continuously in the background. It analyzes data and generates optimized outputs without requiring manual, day-to-day operations input.
  • Highly Scalable: Built on a modular framework, the modules scale elastically alongside your operations as you introduce new tools, volumes and workflows.
